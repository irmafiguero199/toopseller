Music Library — YouTube Music vs Spotify

🎵 Spotify

Spotify has around 100 million tracks, all officially licensed. Its biggest strength is music discovery — Discover Weekly, Daily Mixes, and personalized playlists are genuinely excellent. If finding new artists is important to you, Spotify is hard to beat.

📺 YouTube Music

YouTube Music also has 100M+ tracks — but it goes further. You get live performances, concert recordings, fan covers, acoustic versions, and remixes that simply don't exist on Spotify. If you're a fan who wants every version of a song, YouTube Music wins easily.

Who Wins Each Category?

🏆 For Music Only Users

If you only listen to music and don't watch YouTube videos — Spotify is the better choice. The discovery algorithm is superior, the interface is cleaner for music, and the free tier lets you try before you buy.

🏆 For YouTube + Music Users

If you use YouTube regularly AND want music streaming — YouTube Premium is the clear winner. You get both services in one subscription. No ads on any YouTube video, background play for music, offline downloads for videos and music, and a music library that includes content Spotify doesn't have.

🏆 For Music Fans Who Want Everything

YouTube Music wins for depth of catalog — especially live recordings, remixes, and rare versions that only exist on YouTube.
